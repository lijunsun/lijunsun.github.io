I am an Associate Professor and William Dawson Scholar in the Department of Civil Engineering at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/civil/lijun-sun), where I lead the [Smart Transportation Lab](http://smart.lab.mcgill.ca/). I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Transportation) from the [National University of Singapore](http://www.eng.nus.edu.sg/cee/) and my B.Eng. in Civil Engineering from [Tsinghua University](http://www.civil.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/). During my Ph.D., I worked in the Mobility and Transportation Planning module of the [Future Cities Laboratory](http://www.fcl.ethz.ch/), Singapore-ETH Centre. Prior to joining McGill in 2018, I was a Postdoctoral Associate at the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/).

My research develops statistical and machine learning methods for transportation systems that are only partially observed—incomplete passenger counts, sparse trajectories, censored demand—and for decisions that change what will be observed next. It connects three areas that are often treated separately: inference about latent transportation systems (inverse problems such as origin–destination estimation and passenger flow assignment), structured uncertainty quantification (Bayesian spatiotemporal and low-rank tensor models, probabilistic forecasting with correlated errors), and operational decision-making under uncertainty (public transit operations, Bayesian optimization, surrogate-based optimization). My group is also exploring generative AI and large language model agents for mobility reasoning, synthetic travel data and population synthesis. See [Publications](/publications/) for representative work by theme.

I serve as Managing Editor of [Artificial Intelligence for Transportation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-for-transportation), Area Editor of [Transportation Research Part C](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/transportation-research-part-c-emerging-technologies), and Associate Editor of [Transportation Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/journal/trsc). My research has been recognized by the Best Paper Award of the TRB Standing Committee on Statistical and Econometric Methods (2024), McGill's Christophe Pierre Award for Research Excellence (2023), and the Faculty of Engineering Class of 1944 Outstanding Teaching Award (2022). My work has been featured in Scientific American, WIRED, MIT Technology Review and New Scientist.

Research Interests
======
* Bayesian statistics and uncertainty quantification for transportation systems
* Spatiotemporal modeling, low-rank tensor learning and Gaussian processes
* Probabilistic forecasting of traffic, demand and travel time
* Inverse problems in transportation: origin–destination estimation, passenger flow assignment, unmet demand
* Human mobility, travel behavior and choice modeling
* Population synthesis and generative AI for mobility (synthetic travel data, LLM agents)
* Public transport operations and planning
* Decision-making under uncertainty: Bayesian optimization, surrogate-based and stochastic optimization
* Driver behavior and stochastic traffic simulation
* Shared and active mobility
